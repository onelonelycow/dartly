## Rules, restated because they are easy to break at speed

- **Never send two of these to the same site.**
- **One follow-up maximum**, and leave at least a week.
- **Never offer payment for a link.** It violates Google's guidelines and can be
  penalised.
- Each email above names that specific article and says something true about it.
  Do not swap the bodies between targets.

## Where the list should grow from here

Six cuts leaves this thin. The gap is targets that write roundups but do **not**
run a board — which rules out most of what a search for "best job boards"
returns, since the sites that rank for it usually rank because they run one.

Better seams to search, all untested:
- **Design and creative agencies** publishing career content, like magier.
- **Business-of-freelancing sites** — tax, invoicing, contracts, insurance —
  which serve freelancers without competing for their attention. Lettuce is
  exactly this shape and it is the cleanest target on the list.
- **University and bootcamp career pages** listing where graduates find work.
