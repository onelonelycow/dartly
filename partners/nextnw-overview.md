# Nabbly × Next Northwest

**A proposed in-kind collaboration.** No money moves in either direction.

Prepared for the Next Northwest executive board.

---

## What Nabbly is

Freelance work in marketing, design, writing and video is scattered across
dozens of job boards, newsletters and hiring communities. The person who
answers a good post first is usually the person who gets it — which means the
work goes to whoever happened to be refreshing the right tab at the right
minute, not to whoever was best for it.

Nabbly watches all of those sources continuously and puts every new gig in one
place, the moment it posts. It sorts each one by field, budget and urgency,
matches it against what a member actually does, and can alert them the moment
something fits.

**The board is live now**, carrying thousands of gigs across 20+ fields and
refreshing every couple of minutes, around the clock.

For a Pro member, it goes one step further: it drafts the opening reply from
the real text of the posting, so answering takes seconds instead of starting
from a blank message.

## Why this membership specifically

Nabbly's largest categories are the ones NextNW is built around — marketing and
SEO, design and creative, writing and content, video and animation, brand and
strategy, photography.

More to the point, NextNW's membership includes a lot of people who freelance
or take project work alongside staff roles. That is exactly the group whose
income depends on hearing about work early, and exactly the group that has no
good way to do it today.

## The proposal, in plain terms

### Nabbly provides

- **Pro access, free, for every NextNW member, for a 90-day test.** No cost to
  NextNW and no cost to members. Nothing to cancel and no card required.
- Gigs ranked against each member's own skills.
- A drafted opening reply for any gig, written from the actual post.
- Instant alerts on the channel a member already uses.
- Market-rate context, so members can price against what comparable work pays.
- A direct line for feedback, with fixes shipped during the test rather than
  after it.

### Next Northwest provides

- A listing on the member homepage, alongside other member companies.
- One announcement to the membership, linking to the page below.
- Introductions at upcoming networking events, where many attendees freelance.
- Honest feedback from members who actually use it.
- Permission to reference NextNW as a launch partner, if the test goes well.

### The landing page

**https://nabbly.co/nextnw.html**

A page built for this membership: what the service does, the terms above stated
openly, and a direct way in. Anyone arriving through it is recognised as a
NextNW member, so Pro is unlocked without a code to distribute or a list to
maintain.

That link is also how the collaboration gets measured. Traffic and signups
arriving through it are reported separately, so both sides can see what it
actually produced rather than guessing.

## What each side is really getting

For NextNW, this is a member benefit with no budget attached and no
administration: one announcement, and members get a tool worth real money for
three months.

For Nabbly, it is the thing an early product cannot buy — a concentrated group
of exactly the right professionals, using it properly, and saying what's wrong.

## Being straight about the stage

Nabbly is early, and NextNW would be the first organised group to use it.
That is the offer, and it is not a disclaimer: members get genuine influence
over what a tool for their profession becomes, at the point where that
influence still changes things.

The board is live and stable today. What needs real users to settle is which
fields are still thin, whether the alerts land at the right frequency, and
whether the drafted replies are genuinely good enough to send. Those are the
questions the test is for.

Anyone can browse the whole board free, without an account, before deciding
anything.

## What happens if the board says yes

1. NextNW sends one announcement with the link.
2. Members open it, look around, and sign in only if they want the Pro
   features.
3. Feedback comes back through the app or directly, and gets acted on during
   the test.
4. At 90 days, both sides look at what happened and decide what comes next.

There is no automatic renewal, no invoice at the end, and nothing that has to
be unwound if it doesn't work out.
