Questions

Good to know

Where do the gigs come from?

Public job boards and hiring communities — We Work Remotely, RemoteOK, Remotive, Jobicy, Arbeitnow, Freelancer.com and the hiring subreddits — read continuously and gathered in one place, so you're not keeping ten tabs open.

How fresh are they?

The board refreshes itself every couple of minutes, around the clock. Most gigs show up within minutes of being posted — the whole point is that the person who answers first usually gets the work.

Do I have to sign up?

No. The entire board is free to search and browse without an account. Signing in saves your profile so the board can sort itself around you. Pro is free to try for 14 days whenever you want it, and you choose when to start.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free gives you every gig from every source, plus search and browse. Pro adds the parts that help you reply first: gigs ranked by fit, drafted replies, market-rate data, and instant alerts.

Can I bring in gigs from a newsletter or listserv?

Yes. Every signed-in member gets a private forwarding address. Forward a mailing list or newsletter to it and Nabbly pulls the gigs out and files them on your board — privately, visible only to you.

Are the gigs verified?

No, and be careful. These are public postings gathered as they were written; we classify and rank them, we don't vet the people behind them. Treat anything asking for money up front or unpaid "test work" the way you would anywhere else.
