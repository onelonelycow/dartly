## LinkedIn

(image: 01-introducing.png, or both as a document carousel)

    Introducing Draft Voice.

    Nabbly watches freelance job boards around the clock and puts new gigs in
    one feed the moment they post, across more than 20 fields. It already
    writes your first reply to any of them.

    What it could not do until this week was sound like you.

    Draft Voice adds four controls to your profile. How long the reply runs,
    one line it should always work in, what it should never bring up, and how
    you sign off. Set them once and every draft after that follows.

    The reason it is four boxes and not a text field: the underlying prompt is
    what stops a draft reading as generated, and handing that over would make
    the replies worse. These sit alongside it rather than replacing it.

    Draft Voice is part of Pro. Free accounts still get the draft written for
    them.

    nabbly.co
